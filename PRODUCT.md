# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person: the owner. They track their own money across Korean won (KRW) and Indonesian rupiah (IDR) wallets, almost always on a phone, as an installed PWA. Sessions are short: log a coffee right after paying, glance at what is left this month on the commute, look back over a month in the evening. Data entry happens one-handed and in a hurry; review happens slowly and calmly.

## Product Purpose

A personal ledger that makes it effortless to record income and expenses as they happen, and to see at a glance where each wallet stands this month. Success: logging a transaction takes a few seconds, and opening the app answers "how am I doing this month?" without any digging.

## Brand Personality

Calm, tidy, friendly. Checking money should feel relaxed, never anxious. The interface is orderly and soft-spoken: generous space, rounded surfaces, plain words. Numbers are the content; everything else stays quiet so they can be read instantly.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboard**: gray sidebar, rows of identical stat cards, gradient accents, dense admin tables.
- **Childish or gamified**: cartoon icons, confetti, badges, streaks, mascots.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers first.** Amounts are the most important thing on every screen; layout, type and color exist to make them readable at a glance.
2. **Fast in, calm out.** Adding a transaction is one tap away from anywhere and finishes in seconds; reviewing is unhurried and spacious.
3. **Everything in its place.** Each screen has one job and one obvious primary action. Nothing decorative competes with the data.
4. **Meaning never rides on color alone.** Income and expense are distinguished by sign and label as well as color.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and UI boundaries), visible focus indicators, touch targets at least 44px, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, and income vs expense always marked with a + or − sign, not only color. Light and dark themes both meet these targets.
